I am an engineer and data-scientist currently focused on model based systems engineering and architecture design within industrial manufacturing environment that tackle interoperability and scalability within industry 4.0. My background can be summarized in two folds, one rather model-based where at least some crude models of the problems at hand are available which my academic background also revolves more around, and one rather model-free where problems don't render explicit/tractable models and appreciate data-driven approaches which most of my research and publications involve. Robotics being the core of my background for both the above paradigms has involved two scenarios for me so far: 1: low level and generic software developement and architecture design 2: use-case specific solution design, especially for industrial costomers. The past years how ever my focus has zoomed further out from robotics cells to the overall manufacturing ecosystems and industry 4.0 standards, ranging from topics such digital-twins and communication technologies such as 5G, and interoperability standards such as Factory-X and Catena-X.

In summary, I thrive in interdisciplinary domains, with a strong focus on robotics, software development, data engineering and science, metrology, optimization, and R&D.
