Nov 24, 2022 Publication of US Patent on Reinforcement Learning for Control Systems – My US patent, detailing a novel method for training control arrangements in systems such as robotics, was officially published. I developed an architecture where an actuator, trained via reinforcement learning, corrects the feedback information supplied to a regulation device rather than modifying the output control signals directly. This methodology prevents the regulation device from treating actuator corrections as disturbances, optimizing the overall control strategy. Patent

Dec 01, 2019 High-Dimensional Bayesian Optimization for Soft Microrobots – I completed a specialized research project at the Max Planck Institute for Intelligent Systems, focusing on learning-based control methods for soft microrobots. I investigated the application of high-dimensional Bayesian optimization to learn model-free locomotion policies for light-driven microrobotic systems. By combining Bayesian optimization with dimensionality-reduction techniques and surrogate modeling, I developed strategies to efficiently optimize locomotion speed across complex parameter spaces in simulated environments.
