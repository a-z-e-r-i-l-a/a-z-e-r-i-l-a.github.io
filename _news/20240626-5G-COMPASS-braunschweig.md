5G-COMPASS Project Meeting in Braunschweig – I participated in the fourth 5G-COMPASS project meeting, hosted at the Technical University of Braunschweig. I contributed to the strategic discussions on the development of the project's upcoming technical demonstrators, which will showcase novel communication functionalities—including Wi-Fi, Li-Fi, PLC, and PON technologies—across industrial, medical, office, and home applications.
